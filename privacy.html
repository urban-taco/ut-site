Privacy Policy — Urban Taco GBP Automation

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Urban Taco GBP Automation is an internal application operated by Tasty Food LLC, doing business as Urban Taco ("Urban Taco," "we"), 123 Marietta St NW, Atlanta, GA 30303, USA. Contact: realurbantaco@gmail.com.

What this application does

This application is used by Urban Taco only to manage Urban Taco's own Google Business Profile. It is not offered to the public, is not used on behalf of any third party, and manages a single business location owned by the operator.

Data accessed

With the business owner's authorization, the application accesses the owner's Google Business Profile data through the Google Business Profile APIs, using the single scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage. This data may include business location details and hours, customer reviews, local posts, photos/media metadata, and business performance metrics for Urban Taco's own location.

How the data is used

  • Managing regular and special business hours for Urban Taco's own location.
  • Reading customer reviews and posting replies that the business owner has reviewed and approved.
  • Creating local posts that the business owner has approved.
  • Internal business performance reporting.

Every public change (review reply, post, hours change) requires the business owner's explicit approval before it is published.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties. Data is not used for advertising. Data is not transferred to any other party except as necessary to operate this internal application on infrastructure controlled by Urban Taco.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens are stored only in secured secret storage controlled by Urban Taco and are never published. Business data retrieved through the APIs is retained only as long as needed for the purposes above. The owner may revoke this application's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and may request deletion of stored data by contacting realurbantaco@gmail.com.

Google API Services disclosure

Urban Taco GBP Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes
